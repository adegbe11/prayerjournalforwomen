Prayer Journal for Women

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what the Prayer Journal for Women app does with your information. The short version: we do not collect any of it.

  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app has no internet access. What you write stays on your phone.
  • We, the people who make the app, never receive your data. We have no server that could receive it.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps these things on your phone only:

  • Your weekly pages: reflections, prayers and look back notes
  • Your daily pages, gratitude lists and journal pages
  • Your prayer list, answered prayers and how often you prayed
  • Your journals, tags, fasts and timers
  • Photos and videos you add to pages, handwriting pages, and prayers or sermons you record
  • Your Bible highlights, favourite verses, reading plans and verses you are learning by heart
  • Your people, date night and hospitality notes
  • If you turn it on: your cycle log (period days, what you noticed, and notes)
  • If you turn it on: your pregnancy dates (due date, birth date, baby's name) and letters to your baby
  • Your settings, such as your name, Bible version, reminder times, passcode and theme. Your passcode is stored only as a scrambled code (a salted hash), never as the numbers you typed.

The Bible verses, reflections, questions, related verses and Greek and Hebrew word study are built into the app. They need no internet.

No internet

The app does not ask Android for internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere by itself. The Privacy policy link in the app opens this page in your web browser.

Photos, videos, camera and microphone

When you add a photo or video, Android's photo picker lets you choose which ones the app may use. The app copies only those into your journal and cannot see the rest of your photos and videos. The camera is used only when you tap Camera to take a picture for a page.

The microphone is used only while you record a prayer or a sermon, which you start and stop yourself. So that a sermon keeps recording with the screen off, Android runs the recording as a foreground service and shows a "Recording" notification and its microphone sign until you stop. The recording is saved in your journal on this phone and nowhere else.

Cycle tracker and pregnancy

The cycle tracker and the week-by-week pregnancy companion are off until you turn them on. What you log stays in your journal on this phone, behind your lock, like everything else you write. It is never sent anywhere, never shared, and never used for ads. Dates and baby sizes shown are estimates and general guides, not medical advice, and not for birth control. You can turn either off at any time. In My cycle you can delete your whole cycle log, and stopping the pregnancy companion clears its dates.

Google backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android copies the app's data into your own Google backup, so a new phone can get your journal back. Android only does this when the backup can be encrypted, so only your phone can read it. Photos and recordings are left out of this backup because of its size limit; they move with a phone-to-phone transfer or in your own backup file. This is part of Android, under your Google Account. We cannot see it.

Backup files you save

In Me, "Back up my journal" saves your journal, with its photos and recordings, as one file. You can lock it with a password; without that password nobody can open it, including us. You choose where it goes, for example your Downloads folder or Google Drive. "Restore from a backup" reads a file you pick. The app only touches the file you choose.

Journal lock

You can lock the journal with a passcode of your own, or with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android handles fingerprints and faces. The app only learns whether you unlocked it. It never sees your fingerprint or face data. You can also set a decoy passcode that opens a separate, empty journal. The lock is off until you turn it on.

Notifications

The app can remind you to pray each day, and for any prayer you ask it to, tell you when a fast you set has ended, and shows a notification while a recording is running. These are set up on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Sharing and printing

If you share a verse, a page or your journal as text, the app hands it to Android's share menu. If you print or save as PDF, it hands it to Android's print service. You choose where it goes. The app does not send it anywhere by itself.

Deleting your data

  • Deleted pages and prayers go to Recently deleted for 30 days, then they are removed. You can remove them sooner there.
  • In Me, "Erase my journal" deletes everything you wrote from this phone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts to handle data in a new way, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version is released.
