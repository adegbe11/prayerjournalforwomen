Prayer Journal for Women

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what the Prayer Journal for Women app does with your information. The short version: we do not collect any of it.

  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app has no internet access. What you write stays on your phone.
  • We, the people who make the app, never receive your data. We have no server that could receive it.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps these things on your phone only:

  • Your weekly pages: reflections, prayers and look back notes
  • Your daily pages, gratitude lists and journal pages
  • Your prayer list, answered prayers and how often you prayed
  • Your settings, such as your name, Bible version, reminder times and theme

The Bible verses, reflections and questions are built into the app. They need no internet.

No internet

The app does not ask Android for internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere by itself. The Privacy policy link in the app opens this page in your web browser.

Google backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android copies the app's data into your own Google backup, so a new phone can get your journal back. Android only does this when the backup can be encrypted, so only your phone can read it. This is part of Android, under your Google Account. We cannot see it.

Backup files you save

In Me, "Back up my journal" saves your journal as a file. You choose where it goes, for example your Downloads folder or Google Drive. "Restore from a backup" reads a file you pick. The app only touches the file you choose.

Journal lock

You can lock the journal with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android handles this. The app only learns whether you unlocked it. It never sees your fingerprint or face data. The lock is off until you turn it on.

Notifications

The app can remind you to pray each day, and for any prayer you ask it to. These reminders are set up on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Sharing and printing

If you share a verse, a page or your journal as text, the app hands it to Android's share menu. If you print or save as PDF, it hands it to Android's print service. You choose where it goes. The app does not send it anywhere by itself.

Deleting your data

  • Deleted pages and prayers go to Recently deleted for 30 days, then they are removed. You can remove them sooner there.
  • In Me, "Erase my journal" deletes everything you wrote from this phone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts to handle data in a new way, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version is released.
