Support

Need help with Prayer Journal for Women? Email AseinCollins@gmail.com and we will reply.

Moving to a new phone

In the app, open Me and tap "Back up my journal". Save the file to Google Drive or send it to yourself. On the new phone, install the app, open Me, tap "Restore from a backup" and pick that file.

If Google backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may also bring your journal back by itself when you set up the new phone.

I deleted something by mistake

Open Me and tap "Recently deleted". Pages and prayers stay there for 30 days. Tap Restore to bring one back.

Reminders do not show up

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's settings. Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. If yours does, set the app's battery use to "Unrestricted".

The lock does not open

The lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock. If your fingerprint does not work, use your phone's PIN, pattern or password.

Starting a new year

After 52 weeks, open Me and tap "Start a new year". You get 52 new weeks with new verses. Everything from your first year stays in the Journal tab.
